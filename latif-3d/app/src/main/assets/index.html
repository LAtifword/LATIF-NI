The Mo Pai story

John Chang, often called Dynamo Jack, was featured in documentary accounts of purported internal-energy abilities. His reported tradition emphasizes meditation and the lower dantian, a traditional concept referring to an area of the lower abdomen.

Historical claim: practitioners describe cultivating qi or internal energy. Established observation: attention and breathing practices can influence subjective sensations and some physiological measures. Not established: that such practice enables psychokinesis, ignition by intention, or generation of extraordinary electrical current.

Training concepts

Lower-dantian attention

Gently place attention on sensations in the lower abdomen. This is an attentional anchor, not proof of an anatomical energy reservoir.

Breathing

Observe comfortable, natural breathing. Never force breathing, hyperventilate, hold your breath for prolonged periods, or strain.

Concentration

Notice when attention wanders and return without judgment. Improvement can be tracked by consistency, not supernatural milestones.

Reality-testing laboratory

These exercises demonstrate why good controls matter. They do not teach supernatural abilities.

Experiment A · Moving paper

Place a small paper strip under a clear enclosure. Observe it with the enclosure open and closed. Compare air movement, static electricity and body proximity.

Question: does movement persist when airflow is excluded?

Experiment B · Warmth

Compare subjective warmth during focused attention with a room-temperature reading from an ordinary thermometer. Keep hands still and record the environmental conditions.

Question: does subjective heat correspond to a measured change?

Experiment C · Randomized prediction

Before each coin flip, predict heads or tails. Record at least 30 predictions. Compare hits against the 50% chance expectation. A streak alone is not evidence of an ability.

Prediction required before each flip.

Safety boundaries

Do not attempt to pass electrical current through your body, ignite objects, perform extreme breath retention, or use dangerous physical stress to imitate filmed demonstrations. Stop any exercise that causes dizziness, pain or distress.
